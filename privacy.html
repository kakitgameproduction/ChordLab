Chordasy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

English

Chordasy is a music chart and setlist app. The app stores imported PDFs, JPG, JPEG, and PNG files, parsed song information, setlists, annotations, and settings on your device.

If you connect Dropbox, Chordasy accesses the Dropbox folder you choose so it can read chart files and sync them into the app. If you connect Google Drive, Chordasy requests read-only access to your Google Drive so it can browse folders and read supported chart files. Chordasy only uses this access for the folder you select and does not modify, delete, or upload files to Google Drive.

Chordasy may store limited connected-account information, such as the Google Drive account display name, email address, or account identifier, so it can show which account is connected and reset the selected folder if you connect a different account.

Chordasy does not sell, transfer, or disclose Google user data to third parties for advertising, analytics, or marketing. Google Drive file data is used only inside Chordasy to provide the cloud library features requested by the user. The app may share chart files or metadata only when you intentionally use Band Mode to send them to another Chordasy device on your local network.

Chordasy protects sensitive data by limiting access to the minimum data needed, using read-only Google Drive access, communicating with Google Drive and Dropbox over HTTPS, and storing app data, connected-account details, OAuth tokens, cached chart files, annotations, and settings locally within the app's device storage. You can remove a cloud connection in Settings to stop Chordasy from accessing that cloud account.

Band Mode communicates with other Chordasy devices on the same local network. When you send a chart or message, the app may transmit chart metadata, text, and, when needed, the PDF or image file to the receiving device.

On Android, Chordasy may store Band Mode trial status and Google Play purchase status on your device so Full Version features can be unlocked or restored. Payment processing is handled by Google Play, not by Chordasy.

Chordasy does not operate a separate cloud server for your song library and does not sell personal data. Network, storage, and media permissions are used only to provide the app features described here. For support or data removal requests, please use the contact details on the Chordasy website.

Support: support@chordasy.com

中文

Chordasy 是一個樂譜及 Setlist 管理應用程式。應用程式會將匯入的 PDF、JPG、JPEG、PNG、歌曲資料、Setlist、手寫標記及設定儲存在您的裝置上。

若您連接 Dropbox，Chordasy 會讀取您所選擇的 Dropbox 資料夾，用作同步樂譜檔案到 app 內。若您連接 Google Drive，Chordasy 會要求 Google Drive 讀取權限，以瀏覽資料夾及讀取支援的樂譜檔案。Chordasy 只會將此權限用於您所選擇的資料夾，不會修改、刪除或上載檔案到 Google Drive。

Chordasy 可能會在本機儲存有限的已連接帳戶資料，例如 Google Drive 帳戶顯示名稱、電郵地址或帳戶識別碼，用作顯示目前連接的帳戶，並在您改用另一個帳戶時重設所選資料夾。

Chordasy 不會出售、轉移或向第三方披露 Google 使用者資料作廣告、分析或市場推廣用途。Google Drive 檔案資料只會在 Chordasy 內用作提供使用者要求的雲端資料庫功能。只有當您主動使用 Band Mode 將樂譜傳送到同一本地網絡上的另一部 Chordasy 裝置時，app 才可能分享樂譜檔案或相關資料。

Chordasy 會透過限制只存取必要資料、使用 Google Drive 只讀權限、以 HTTPS 與 Google Drive 及 Dropbox 通訊，並將 app 資料、已連接帳戶資料、OAuth token、快取樂譜檔案、手寫標記及設定儲存在裝置上的 app 儲存空間內，以保護敏感資料。您可以在 Settings 移除雲端連接，以停止 Chordasy 存取該雲端帳戶。

Band Mode 會與同一個本地網絡上的 Chordasy 裝置通訊。當您傳送歌曲或訊息時，應用程式可能會傳送歌曲資料、文字內容，以及在需要時傳送 PDF 或圖片檔案到對方裝置。

在 Android 裝置上，Chordasy 可能會在本機儲存 Band Mode 試用狀態及 Google Play 購買狀態，以便啟用或還原 Full Version 功能。付款流程由 Google Play 處理，並非由 Chordasy 處理。

Chordasy 不會營運額外雲端伺服器儲存您的歌曲資料庫，亦不會出售個人資料。網絡、儲存空間及媒體權限只會用作提供上述功能。如需支援或資料移除協助，請使用 Chordasy 網站上的聯絡方式。

支援電郵：support@chordasy.com
